Privacy Policy — MotoHub Rider (Customer Application)

Application ID: com.bossarman.rider • Version v1.0 (2026-10) • Effective Date: October 30, 2026 • Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the MotoHub Rider Customer Application (com.bossarman.rider) for Boss Arman MotoHub in Santa Cruz, Laguna. We process your personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012).

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: First name, last name, username, email address, optional profile photo, salted password hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) or Google Sign-In identifier, and your Terms & Conditions acceptance version and timestamp.
  • Service Booking & Vehicle Information: Contact phone number, motorcycle model, license plate number, service type, issue description, preferred branch (Patimbao or Pedro Guevara), and preferred schedule.
  • Click & Collect Reservations: Reserved part SKU/name, quantity, pickup branch, contact phone number, and reservation status.
  • Customer Support Chat: Text messages and optional photo attachments sent to Boss Arman MotoHub staff.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token stored in user_bindings to send live repair and reservation alerts.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Creating and authenticating your customer account via Email/Password or Google One-Tap Sign-In.
  • Reserving genuine motorcycle parts and holding stock at your chosen branch for Click & Collect pickup.
  • Scheduling service appointments, tracking live bay repair status, and requesting your approval for optional extra repairs.
  • Delivering real-time push notifications and enabling direct chat support with shop staff.

4. Cloud Services, Data Sharing & Security

The application uses local SQLite caching (sqflite) and Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, and Cloud Messaging). All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Boss Arman MotoHub does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Self-Service Account Deletion & Data Retention

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account at any time in the app via My Garage → Delete Account.
  • Immediately Deleted Data: Your customer account profile (users and customer_accounts), Firebase Authentication record, profile photo, and FCM device bindings (user_bindings).
  • Retained Business Records: Completed in-store Official Receipts (POS transactions) and store accounting ledgers are retained as anonymized business records for tax and audit compliance under Philippine law.

6. Your Rights & Contact Information

Under RA 10173, you have the right to be informed, access, correct, object, and request deletion of your personal data.
