Privacy Policy — BossArman MotoHub (Admin & Staff ERP)

Application ID: com.bossarman.motohub • Version v1.0 (2026-10) • Effective Date: October 30, 2026 • Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the BossArman MotoHub Admin & Staff ERP Application (com.bossarman.motohub) operated for Boss Arman MotoHub branches in Santa Cruz, Laguna (Branch 1: Patimbao and Branch 2: Pedro Guevara Ave.). It explains how personal, employee, customer, operational, and device data are collected, processed, stored, and protected in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012).

2. Information Collected & Processed

  • Employee & Staff Account Data: First name, last name, username, email address, role (Admin, Cashier, Mechanic), assigned branch, shift status, optional profile photo, and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 salted password hashes.
  • Authentication Data: Firebase Authentication UIDs and Google Sign-In profile identifiers (email, display name, photo URL) when authorized staff sign in via Google One-Tap.
  • Customer Service & Reservation Records: Customer full name, username, phone number, motorcycle model, plate number, service issue descriptions, repair bay status, attached parts, and Click & Collect orders.
  • POS & Financial Audit Data: Official Receipt (OR) numbers, itemized sales, labor charges, payment method (Cash, GCash, Split), last 4+ digits of GCash reference numbers, optional GCash payment proof images, cash drawer float/cashout logs, and Z-Reading end-of-shift reports.
  • Support Chat Messages: Text messages and photo attachments exchanged with customers in the in-app support chat.
  • Device & Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens stored in user_bindings to deliver real-time shop alerts.
  • Camera & Media Access: Camera permission is used strictly on-demand to scan part barcodes/QR codes and capture optional profile or payment proof images.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

  • Enforcing role-based access control (RBAC) and branch assignments across Patimbao and Pedro Guevara branches.
  • Synchronizing real-time inventory stock, locked part holds, job orders, and Click & Collect reservations across authorized devices.
  • Generating accurate cash drawer ledgers, shift audits, and Z-Reading reports.
  • Delivering real-time push notifications for incoming orders, service bookings, stock transfers, and customer chat inquiries.

4. Database, Cloud Storage & Third-Party Services

The application uses an offline-first local SQLite database (sqflite) synchronized with Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging). We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Security, Retention & Account Deletion

  • Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and all cloud traffic is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Deleted staff accounts enter a 30-day soft-delete recovery window before permanent removal, or can be permanently removed immediately by an Administrator.
  • FCM device tokens are unbound automatically upon logout or account deletion.
  • Historical POS receipts, cash drawer entries, and Z-Readings are retained as business accounting records in compliance with Philippine tax and commercial laws.

6. Data Subject Rights & Contact Information

Under Republic Act No. 10173, data subjects have the right to be informed, access, rectify, object, and request erasure or blocking of personal data.
