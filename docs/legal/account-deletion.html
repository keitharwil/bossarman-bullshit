Account & Data Deletion Request — Boss Arman MotoHub

Applies to MotoHub Rider (com.bossarman.rider) & BossArman MotoHub ERP (com.bossarman.motohub)

Option 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your MotoHub Rider customer account immediately inside the mobile app:

  1. Open the MotoHub Rider app and sign in.
  2. Go to the My Garage (Profile) tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password (for Email/Password accounts) or type DELETE (for Google Sign-In accounts) and tap Verify & Delete.

Option 2: Web / Email Account Deletion Request (Without App Installed)

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, send an account deletion request from your registered email address:

Request Account Deletion via Email (bossarman@motohub.ph)

Verified external deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted Immediately: Your user profile document (users and customer_accounts), Firebase Authentication login credentials, profile photo, and FCM push notification device bindings (user_bindings).
  • Retained for Legal & Tax Audit Compliance: Historical Point-of-Sale Official Receipts (OR-*), cash drawer ledger entries, and end-of-shift Z-Reading financial reports are retained as anonymized store accounting records in compliance with Philippine tax and commercial laws.
